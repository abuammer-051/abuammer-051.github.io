Privacy policyContact support

Sketchscape is being prepared for Google Play. The core game works offline; ads and purchases need internet access. No game account is required. Progress and settings stay on your device.

Sketchscape Plus is a one-time purchase that removes ads and includes three ad-free revives per Endless run and one +2-life claim per Story attempt. Regional age ratings are provided by Google Play. Ads are disabled for Under 18 and Prefer not to say age selections.
